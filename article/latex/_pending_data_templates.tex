\subsection{External baseline + module-swap comparison}
\label{sec:external-swap}

To address whether the EDO mechanisms add value on top of independent multi-agent systems --- not just on top of our own Stage-1 baselines --- we run a controlled module-swap comparison on three open-source 2024 systems (AutoGen, ChatEval, MAD). For each host we hold the host's prompts, agent count, retrieval stack, and termination policy fixed and only swap a single decision component to one of our R-x mechanisms.

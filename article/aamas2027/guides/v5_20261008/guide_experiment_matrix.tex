{\Large\bfseries When Should \rare{} Win---and Lose?}\par
\caution
This v5 guide repairs the link between the proposed mechanism and the paper-facing
experiment tables. It keeps the v4 benchmark--baseline closure contract and adds an
explicit regime-by-contrast direction matrix. A direction is eligible for confirmation
only after its premise, read cut, common evaluation population, cost envelope and
falsifier are frozen on a development stream or by an exogenous mutation. Conditions
must never be named after seeing the confirmation result.

\lead{Status and interpretation}
The current status is \texttt{CURRENT\_CONDITIONAL\_DIRECTION\_GUIDE\_PENDING\_EVIDENCE}.
The current evidence has no qualified confirmation roots, complete live baseline parity,
independent future assignment outcomes, or scientific closure receipt. The v1 numerical
targets were withdrawn and cannot be reused. The synthetic receipt tests validate only
the software contract, not this matrix or a method effect.

$>$ & \rare{} should be better than the named baseline on this endpoint & Only after the premise and parity contract are sealed.\\

\lead{Four independent endpoints}
H1 is Brier/log loss on the same already-selected held-out cohort (lower is better),
with producer contract $Q_p$ and later-use $Y$ kept separate. H2 is independent
future-assignment utility $U=Y-\lambda C$ (higher is better), with all-attempt ITT
denominators and UNKNOWN bounds. H3 is full publish/read/update p95, backlog and
drop/timeout rate under a fixed arrival and resource envelope (lower is better).
H4a is false producer attribution with independent owner truth (lower is better).
Coverage, abstention, UNKNOWN and owner-truth recall are H4b companion quantities;
abstention cannot be used to manufacture an H4a win.

\lead{Mandatory factorial and locality blocks}
Evidence and delayed credit are one four-arm matched factorial: (i) neither,
(ii) public evidence only, (iii) delayed credit only, (iv) both. The interaction
$I_U=U_{11}-U_{10}-U_{01}+U_{00}$ uses the qualified stream covariance. Locality
requires local, nonlocal/global, and same-cost centralized diagnostics. Graph-IPD is
an external native cooperation reference and cannot fill these cells without a
qualified adapter. Updater comparisons (RLS, online SGD/logistic, periodic refit)
are nested under the same information policy; they are not silently omitted from a
claim about update speed or recovery.

\lead{Stability and safety definitions}
Old-root forgetting is the drop from the pre-drift held-out performance on the old
root. Recovery delay is time/steps to regain a predeclared threshold on the new root.
They are separate endpoints. State bytes is a resource axis, not a success direction:
lineage-safe provenance can require more state. False attribution, false negatives,
coverage, abstention and UNKNOWN must be reported jointly, stratified by producer-only,
recipient-only, sink-only, mixed ownership and resource failure.

\lead{Closure rule}
The v4 B1--B7 receipt remains the sole benchmark+baseline closure rule. Guide alignment
is necessary and not sufficient. The scientific gate is \texttt{OPEN} until all required
roots, live baseline parity, independent streams, future-$Y$ cells, complete tables,
statistics/cost, clean replay and independent review are present and verified. A full
positive, neutral or adverse result can close the evidence gate; an incomplete,
\texttt{UNSTUDIED} or findings-ready result cannot. A closed evidence gate never turns
a null or adverse method result into a positive claim.

# v5 review record

This file records read-only Codex reviews of the v5 conditional direction matrix. It
is not the independent scientific closure review required by B7.

## What the reviews establish

The benchmark/baseline audit found that the current activation manifests still contain
blocked one-root candidates, no live parity for the strongest controls, and no actual
future-Y stream. It required task-conditioned ridge, J-masked/trace-only controls, a
single evidence×delayed-credit factorial, local/global locality controls, updater
comparators, complete cost and ITT/UNKNOWN denominators. It also flagged that topology
labels and renamed seeds do not create structural roots.

The effect-consistency audit found that the current C1 evidence is a trace-explained
diagnostic, not a favorable method result. It required separate H1 prediction, H2
future utility, H3 service, H4 attribution, old-root forgetting and recovery endpoints.
It rejected `assignment change` as a success direction and required common held-out
populations or qualified randomized transport for Brier/log loss.

The method-boundary audit found that v5 is directionally acceptable only as a conditional
guide. RARE may beat weak controls when typed evidence is incrementally informative and
local support is sufficient; it should tie or lose under constant, stale, sparse,
trace-explained or high-cost regimes. The closest published adapter remains NO-GO until
live parity is qualified.

## What the reviews do not establish

No reviewer observed new API calls, GPU runs, qualified roots, future outcomes, complete
result tables, statistical/cost receipts, clean replay, or the required independent
scientific closure. No numeric method ranking is supplied or implied.

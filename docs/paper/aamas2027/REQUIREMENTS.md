## 2. Recommended scientific entry and design status

**How can a team admit newly acquired capabilities into reusable workflows without exhaustively retesting every executor combination or accepting harmful collaboration regressions?** A locally improved skill need not improve every collaboration. This is the recommended coordination question; its existence and practical importance in our environments remain to be measured.

The three retained commitments now have specific functions: private experience produces candidate capabilities; contribution evidence qualifies a role in a collaboration context; workflows retain executable handoff requirements independently of fixed agent names. The prospective contribution is learning requirements that help qualify **unobserved combinations** at useful cost. Generic coevolution, pairwise synergy, executable contracts and local repair are prior art.

### 2.1 AAMAS fit and scientific obligations

The official call includes interaction protocols, teamwork, self-improving agents and recovery. GAAI remains the provisional area because the execution and adaptation depend on generative agents. A coordination question and a small explanatory mechanism are our editorial recommendation, not a verified acceptance predictor. AAMAS does not require proving that a centralized controller could never simulate the method.

| Dimension | Required evidence / decision |
|---|---|
| Significance | Naturally learned updates create consequential compatibility problems after competent interface standardization |
| Originality | Useful transferable coordination requirements beyond pairwise matching, consumer-driven integration tests and collaborative self-evolution |
| Soundness | Adequate independent local quality; same-distribution partner comparison; lawful qualification feedback; no unique-blame claim from an arbitrary repair |
| Identification | Real old/new package interventions, unchanged-state controls, equal test resources and held-out package/consumer combinations |
| Generality | Transfer beyond observed pairs; workflow-composition transfer only with an explicit split and actual results |
| Efficiency | Count acquisition, retained versions, qualification, diagnostics, coordination and execution |
| Clarity | One adoption problem; skills, roles and workflows form one causal chain, not three unverified innovations |
| Scope | Reject the entry if a competent common interface cheaply removes the effect, or if ordinary pairwise matching/testing explains all benefit |

### 2.2 Candidate mechanism, not an executable lock

Retain old/new acquired packages. From lawful training traces, propose a bounded source-grounded handoff requirement or procedure, validate it against downstream execution, and store its applicability and qualification cases with the workflow. New packages earn their own support using those reusable cases; no evidence counts are transported to changed packages as if behavior were unchanged. Adopt conditionally, retain a previous working combination, or use a separately validated handoff change. An UNKNOWN check or missing condition is not competence.

The central test is whether learned requirements generalize across unobserved combinations and reduce qualification cost without losing useful task performance. Exact fingerprints remain provenance. A fixed two-PASS rule, v5's stability preference and marginal package ranking are no longer active scientific commitments. The predicate/handoff grammar, test-selection rule, feedback access, adoption criterion and transfer split must be specified before new method execution. Training replay requires faithful isolated worlds and charged access for all arms; no test-world rewind is granted.

### 2.5 Literature boundary

[Modeling and Learning Synergy (AAMAS 2012)](https://www.ifaamas.org/Proceedings/aamas2012/papers/5B_4.pdf) already models capability and interaction effects. [Updates in Human-AI Teams (AAAI 2019)](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/4087) already studies improved models disrupting teamwork. [Pact](https://docs.pact.io/) supplies established consumer-driven integration testing. [Learning-based Assume-Guarantee Regression Verification (CAV 2016)](https://homepage.iis.sinica.edu.tw/~bywang/papers/cav16.pdf) learns/reuses contextual assumptions for evolving systems using exact symbolic models and a mechanical teacher. Merely learning/reusing handoff requirements is therefore insufficient novelty; the finite-feedback stochastic black-box procedure is the missing candidate distinction. Q1 also records earlier assumption learning and learning-enabled component verification. [SkillMAS](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09341v1) and [Meta-Team](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.29790v1) further narrow coevolution/downstream-feedback novelty; their inspected preprints are not asserted to be accepted papers. Trace-based assurance and counterfactual replay are also close component precedents. The [new source cache](../../../references/aamas/problem_entry_20260922/sources.json) records successful and failed access; Q1 gives the inspected scope and candidate distinction.

### A-R03 — Identifiable acquired capabilities (Scientific, P0)

## 5. Paper shape and stop rules

Suggested eight-page content budget, to adjust after results: introduction/problem 1.0; closest work 0.6; model/protocol 1.7; analysis 0.5; experiments/results 3.2; discussion/limitations/conclusion 1.0. References are additional. This is a project layout suggestion, not a required venue structure. Lead with the supported contribution, not the repair history.

Expected central evidence: (1) quality versus full cost with close/pooled comparators, (2) acquired capability profiles and state interventions, (3) native-family transfer, composition-operator intervention and a feedback/regression boundary, and (4) a real trace-to-workflow-to-reuse example. These can share figures/tables. Do not demand a fixed dataset/baseline count, universal SOTA, positive ablations, strategic-agent theory or a new convergence theorem merely to resemble a 'best paper'.

Before freezing the abstract, choose the supported route:

- **Mechanism contribution:** useful acquired capabilities and reusable/composable organization survive matched-budget state, transfer and simple-baseline controls, with a distinction from close prior work. Stronger interaction/emergence wording needs its own defined evidence.
- **Analysis/negative contribution:** a reproducible, nontrivial failure phenomenon with explanatory interventions and scientific significance; rewrite the paper around that finding.
- **Insufficient contribution:** only old router gains, trivial identities, or an underpowered null remain. Continue research or move the cycle; formatting and Findings do not repair missing soundness or significance.

No silent waiver of a failed requirement. Record evidence, revised scope and independent review in the task ledger before changing the claim or release gate.

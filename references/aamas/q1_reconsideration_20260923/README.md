# Q1 reconsideration: nearby primary sources

Retrieved 2026-09-23 from the authors' arXiv HTML versions. The exact downloaded
bytes and URLs are listed in `sources.json`; this is a targeted overlap check,
not an exhaustive priority search or an assertion of acceptance at a venue.

- `roles_with_rails_v1.html` (`arXiv:2605.28433v1`): typed role cards, credit
  estimation, a communication DAG and contract-preserving role evolution. This
  challenges any claim that credit-ranked roles, role contracts or persistent
  organization edits are novel by themselves. Its inspected evaluation uses
  task-level training and held-out items; that is not automatically a held-out
  **combination of privately acquired execution packages and subworkflows**.
- `skill_composer_v1.html` (`arXiv:2606.32025v1`): selects a subset, count and
  order of skills into an executable sequence. This challenges a generic
  "workflow/skill composition" novelty claim. Its described task is single-agent
  skill-plan prediction; do not infer that it answers the present multi-agent
  evidence-transfer question without a closer method comparison.
- `skillcomposer_evolve_v1.html` (`arXiv:2606.06079v1`): a *different*
  SkillComposer paper, with create/improve/merge skill evolution and reported
  AppWorld evaluation. It challenges claiming that experience-derived skill
  creation/merging on AppWorld is novel on its own. Do not conflate it with
  `arXiv:2606.32025` or infer it studies evidence-earned multi-agent roles.

These sources should be compared with the project's existing SkillMAS, Meta-Team,
AgentNet, AWM and synergy-learning audits before any novelty claim is frozen.

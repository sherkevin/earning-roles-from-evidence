AAMAS 2027 - Reciprocal Reviewer Policy
Reciprocal Reviewer Policy
The 26th International Conference on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems
Each submission must designate at least one qualified author who agrees to serve as a reviewer for AAMAS.
The designated author must meet the qualification criteria defined in the AAMAS Reviewer Guidelines.
Exceptions
A submission is exempt from this requirement if:
None of the authors is qualified under the “Reviewer Eligibility section” in the Reviewer Guidelines; or
All qualified authors are serving as Area Chairs (ACs), Senior Program Committee (SPC), or in other official organizing roles for AAMAS 2027.
How to Satisfy the Requirement
During abstract submission, authors must:
Designate an author who will serve as a reviewer; or
Declare that the submission qualifies for an exemption.
Submissions that fail to satisfy this requirement may be desk-rejected.
How This Requirement Is Enforced
Authors meeting the threshold will be contacted by the organizing committee with further instructions.Authors whose submission qualifies for an exemption must select the corresponding exemption declaration in the OpenReview abstract submission form. The Program Chairs may contact authors for clarification if needed.
Enforcement and Sanctions
AAMAS reserves the right to reject submissions that do not satisfy the reciprocal reviewer policy, including cases where designated reviewers fail to complete their assigned reviews.

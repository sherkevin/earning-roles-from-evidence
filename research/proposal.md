# Proposal card — ArtifactRole responsibility evidence

## Problem

In a dependency-bearing workflow, a rejection or failed terminal score can arise from a
producer defect, recipient-owned integration work, or a downstream failure. A scalar reputation
signal cannot identify which responsibility should change.

## Mechanism

Bind the recipient judgment to a versioned delivery and explicit action. Publish a typed noisy
observation after the source episode. Derive producer evidence only from a separate structural
owner gate. At a later read cut, let a legal owner use the public snapshot to seal a future
assignment before execution. Apply one delayed update only after an independent target outcome
and reward contract pass.

## Falsifiable claims

- H1: situated judgment adds predictive information beyond raw acceptance and terminal-only
  signals on independent producer or later-use outcomes.
- H2: the public evidence changes future assignment before target execution and improves unseen
  root quality/cost under matched information and budget.
- H3: typed publication and delayed update meet the pre-registered latency, cost, drift and
  forgetting limits without unsafe attribution.

## Closest alternatives

Uniform, no-update, raw acceptance, terminal-only, same-information contextual trust/bandit,
pooled controller, and a faithful closest published adapter. The adapter may receive an
explicit NO-GO row if it cannot preserve the causal unit and public information contract.

## Current decision

Keep this as the primary paper direction. Treat all present results as protocol/engineering
evidence until H1-H3 are executed with real API calls and raw logs.

# AAMAS Research Charter — 2026-10-09

## Objective

Build and verify an AAMAS paper about whether a real recipient's situated judgment of a
specific delivered artifact can become responsibility-safe evidence for a producer's future
assignment. The paper must connect delivery, recipient use, attribution, pre-execution
assignment, and unseen quality/cost under a reproducible benchmark and baseline contract.

## Venue and current stage

- Venue: AAMAS 2027 Main Track, provisional GAAI area.
- Current stage: B/M closure with engineering qualification evidence; E scientific execution is
  not open.
- Active specifications: `docs/research/canonical/active_versions.json` (registry 1.10).
- Goal authority: `docs/coordination/GOAL.md` v1.0.

## Candidate claim

In dependency-bearing multi-agent workflows, a recipient's judgment becomes useful role
evidence only when it is bound to a concrete delivery, separated from recipient-owned work,
made legal at a future read cut, and tested through a later assignment and independent outcome.
The claim remains a hypothesis until same-information controls show information value and
closed-loop quality/cost value on independent roots.

## Required evidence

1. Real producer and recipient actions on at least two structurally independent roots.
2. Typed judgment, action, producer contract, terminal outcome, attribution and `UNKNOWN`
   records with event-time lineage.
3. Assignment sealed before target execution, with propensity and selected-only accounting.
4. Uniform, no-update, raw-acceptance, terminal-only, same-information contextual,
   pooled-controller, closest published adapter and candidate arms.
5. Out-of-sample information value, later assignment change, unseen-root quality/cost,
   latency, drift response, forgetting, failure and `UNKNOWN` denominators.

## Kill criteria

Stop efficacy expansion and preserve the negative result if situated judgment adds no
pre-registered information beyond the controls, does not change a legal future assignment,
does not improve unseen quality/cost, or is matched by the same-information contextual arm.
Also stop if recipient-only work is credited to a producer, future outcomes leak into a read
cut, `UNKNOWN` is treated as a label, or update latency/cost/forgetting violates the card.

## Evidence boundary

Zero-call fixtures, replay qualifications, schema tests and PDF checks support engineering or
protocol claims. They do not support scientific efficacy, benchmark freeze, method superiority,
or A800 results. Every empirical claim in a submission draft must point to raw JSONL and a
processed result file.

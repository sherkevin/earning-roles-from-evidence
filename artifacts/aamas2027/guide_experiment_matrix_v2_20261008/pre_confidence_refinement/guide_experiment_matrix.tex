{\Large\bfseries Confidence, Joint Distributions and Errors}\par\status
This v2 replaces v1's independently assigned method means and error assumptions.
It gives a mathematically coherent \emph{conditional reference}, not a fitted
prediction of RARE or any baseline. Scientists can test metric relationships
and error patterns against it, while recording which assumptions fail.

All three live arms chose the same peer and reused the same artifact. Their

\lead{One joint model, one selected outcome}
In each task, candidate $H$ has assumed success probability $.7$ and $L$ has
$.3$. Task context swaps their peer identities. These are latent \emph{evaluation}
roles, never oracle information supplied to the selector. Reduced-form routing
skill $\kappa$ means probability of choosing $H$ is $(1+\kappa)/2$.
For the chosen peer only, $Y\in\{0,1\}$ is an independent terminal outcome.
\[
 Q=\mathbb E[Y]=.5+.2\kappa,\qquad
 C=\frac{1+h+(1-Y)}{1.5}+\xi,\qquad U=Y-.1C.
\]
Case: $h=.02$ is assumed policy overhead; failure adds one repair-cost unit.
The baseline expected cost is 1.5 units. Jitter $\xi$ takes $\pm.1$ normalized
cost units with equal probability, independently of $Y$. Thus quality and
cost are related through failure, rather than separate invented columns.
All these parameters are \emph{unestimated assumptions}.

\[
 \operatorname{Var}(\bar U)=\operatorname{Var}(\bar Q)
 +.1^2\operatorname{Var}(\bar C)-.2\operatorname{Cov}(\bar Q,\bar C).
\]
Case: the negative quality--cost covariance widens utility variability relative
to an independence calculation, because a failure reduces quality and adds
repair cost simultaneously.

\lead{How the prediction and error distributions are calculated}
For a chosen type with success rate $q$ and fixed forecast $p$,
\[
 \mathbb E[(p-Y)^2]=q(1-q)+(p-q)^2.
\]
Case: S3 selects $.7$ with probability $.75$ and $.3$ with probability $.25$;
forecasts are $.75$ and $.35$. Both conditional Brier means are
$.21+.05^2=.2125$. The resulting selected success rate is $.60$, not the
$.50$ prevalence of an unselected balanced population.

For $N=30$, integrate $N_H\sim\operatorname{Bin}(30,.75)$, followed by
$S_H\sim\operatorname{Bin}(N_H,.7)$ and
$S_L\sim\operatorname{Bin}(30-N_H,.3)$. Then
\[
 \widehat{\mathrm{ECE}}=
 \frac{|.75N_H-S_H|+|.35(30-N_H)-S_L|}{30}.
\]
Case: each occupied forecast bin contributes its absolute forecast/count error;
empty bins contribute zero. Finite-sample ECE has an upward absolute-value bias,
so its expected sample value exceeds population ECE .05. For S0 both forecast
types merge into one .5 bin; treating them as two bins would be incorrect.
The calculation enumerates binomial counts and jitter counts exactly. It does
not generate fake agent predictions, real API labels, or synthetic paper results.

\lead{Paired errors: outcome coupling cannot be assumed away}\status
Compare S3 with S2 on the same frozen task/quality population. Couple route
draws for this analytical sensitivity only. The chosen peer differs with
probability $D=(.50-.25)/2=.125$, and
$\Delta Q=0.050$, $\Delta U=0.0533$ when overhead is equal.
Let $u=\Pr(Y_H=1,Y_L=1)$; its feasible range is $[0,.3]$.
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(\Delta Y)=.125(1-2u)-.05^2,
 \quad\operatorname{Var}(\Delta U)=
 (1+.1/1.5)^2\operatorname{Var}(\Delta Y)+2(.1)^2(.1)^2.
\]
Case: the final term is specific to independent Rademacher cost jitters.
Correlated jitters require the covariance term; merely bounded jitter does not
justify this equality. The potential outcomes are evaluation assumptions;
unselected outcomes never enter learning feedback.

The shared-potential-outcome assumption applies only to this frozen-policy

candidate artifacts, tasks, costs and missingness; the same RNG seed does not
make their realized outcomes paired counterfactuals.

\lead{Interaction and safety targets must describe observable relationships}
For evidence/credit cells, define $I_U=U_{11}-U_{10}-U_{01}+U_{00}$.
Its variance is $c^\top\Sigma c$ with $c=(1,-1,-1,1)$ and the cells' joint
stream covariance; four independent guessed SDs cannot replace $\Sigma$.
In this model $I_U=(1+.1/1.5)(.2)I_\kappa-(.1/1.5)I_h$.
Case: additive routing and overhead imply zero interaction. A utility interaction
target .02 with $I_h=0$ requires $I_\kappa=0.09375$;
it is a testable skill requirement, not an already predicted gain.

Fail-closed producer credit is a protocol requirement. Its real violation rate
and owner-oracle coverage need independent negative cases, UNKNOWN denominators
and root/stream-aware error analysis. V1's .25/.15/.10/.05 ungated rates are
withdrawn as unsupported expectations. A core timing of .297 ms also cannot
identify p95. A 5 ms maximum envelope would drain four events in 20 ms, but that
is a conditional queue implication; full service and tail measurements remain
required, including backbone/retrieval and publish/read costs.

\lead{Root and history variation can overturn apparent precision}\status
Use $u=.21$ only as an independent-potential-outcome sensitivity point.
Let $\rho$ be the ICC of \emph{paired episode utility differences}; for 30
exchangeable episodes, $DE=1+29\rho$ and
$\sigma_{\rm stream}^2=\operatorname{Var}(\Delta U)DE/30$.
With three roots and six independent streams per root,
\[
 \operatorname{SE}^2(\overline{\Delta U})=
 \frac{\sigma_{\rm root}^2}{3}+\frac{\sigma_{\rm stream}^2}{18}.
\]
Case: stream variance already incorporates episodes and dependence; do not
divide by 30 again. Root variance reflects differing method effects across
tasks; increasing episodes within three roots cannot remove it.

values must be specified before confirmation. No measured variance is available.

\lead{What scientists should collect before calibrating a forecast}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\item Qualify structural roots, scorer/owner truth and baselines first. Freeze
  predicted-success labels separately from propensities and channel eligibility.
\item In a bounded real development pilot, preserve root/stream/task identity,
  before-selection state, predictions, choice probabilities, diverse legal J,
  actual action, independent $Q_p/Y$, complete costs, timestamps and UNKNOWN.
  Each arm has its own realized history.
\item Estimate means \emph{and} joint stream covariance, quality--cost dependence,
  paired differences, autocorrelation, missingness and cross-root heterogeneity.
  A fixed all-accept source cannot identify judgment-content information.
\item Fit prospective distributions using development data only. Validate on
  future held-out streams/roots: bias, 80\%/95\% interval coverage and width,
  calibration and negative/failed cases. Insufficient coverage means the forecast
  lacks confidence even if its mean happens to match.
\item Freeze confirmation estimands, roots, budgets and stopping rules after the
  forecast is calibrated. Do not manufacture narrow errors or lower the Goal
  to make a negative result agree with a guide.
\end{enumerate}
Until those observations exist, method means, rankings and error distributions
remain unestimated. The equations and count laws have checked conditional
validity; they do not certify that the assumed world describes the benchmark.
This guide changes neither the method nor the acceptance standards, and uses
zero new API calls or GPU jobs. All v1 artifacts remain archived.

# Natural-language figure specification

## Figure 1 — causal spine

A wide left-to-right five-stage spine. Stage 1 is a producer's versioned delivery. Stage 2 is a recipient using the delivery and emitting a judgment plus changed-path evidence. Stage 3 is a responsibility gate with two outcomes: producer-attributable evidence continues forward; UNKNOWN or recipient-owned work goes to audit only. Stage 4 is a public evidence snapshot at a read cut. Stage 5 is a sealed future assignment followed by an independent quality/cost outcome. A dashed arrow from the later outcome returns to the next read cut and is labeled delayed selected-only update. The figure must make clear that feedback cannot edit a sealed assignment.

## Figure 2 — information lanes

Three horizontal lanes share an x-axis. EPISODE records producer artifact, recipient judgment, and target outcome. PUBLIC EVIDENCE projects typed public fields, applies the ownership gate, and writes a versioned row; UNKNOWN is a downward audit branch. LOCAL DECISION reads only eligible rows, seals an assignment with its propensity before task start, and performs a selected-only update only for the matching target. Only three cross-lane arrows are allowed: judgment → typed projection, versioned row → read-cut state before selection, and target outcome → selected-only update after the seal.

## Figure 3 — evaluation logic

A top ribbon states the matched contract: same candidate menu, same lawful feedback schedule, same complete-cost budget. Panel (a) separates ArtifactRole (producer attribution) from PeerSelect (local selection and drift). Panel (b) shows no update, raw acceptance, contextual trust, and RARE under the same read cut and target outcome. Panel (c) maps the four pre-registered endpoints to information, assignment quality/cost, online service, and safety/attribution. Arrows label adapter qualification and stream-level comparison.

5. The claim that fixed topology is an inductive bias rather than a limitation needs stronger justification.
6. The "published neighbor self-belief" mechanism - nodes only see neighbors' self-reported competence, which could be miscalibrated. The system doesn't have a way to verify these beliefs independently.

713      A fourth limitation concerns backbone-sensitive
714   method ordering. On our current 200-sample
715   HotpotQA chain slice, the relative ranking
716   of Stage-1 delegation methods inverts between
717   glm-4-flash and gpt-4.1-mini. On the stronger
718   backbone, fixed_self_claim leads F1, while
719   fixed_peer_calibrated trails. This indicates
720   that the TCPB peer-calibration overhead (addi-
721   tional LLM calls for belief updates) does not yield
722   F1 gains when the underlying model is already
723   strong enough to self-evaluate accurately. This
724   result is theoretically important: it predicts that
725   the full EDO organizational advantage will be
726   most visible on harder tasks, weaker individual
727   agents, or richer topologies where single-agent self-
728   assessment breaks down. Reporting Stage-1 results
729   on only one backbone would conceal this sensi-
730   tivity; we report both for transparency. A related
